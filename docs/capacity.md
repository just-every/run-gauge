**Capacity** shows next month before it starts: for each person, their available, committed, tentative and remaining time, with any conflicts flagged.

## Calendars and leave

Finance adds a **Working calendar / contractor availability** for each person: the dates it covers, the available hours per working day, the working weekdays (Monday to Friday are ticked to start) and the timezone. A contractor's calendar records the availability they told you about.

Anyone can add their own **Leave**; finance can add leave for anyone. Other people see that leave was taken, not the reason.

## Staffing

A lead (or finance) adds a **Staff allocation**: the engagement, the person, the dates, the hours per date and whether it is **tentative** or **committed**. Allocations apply to every date in their range.

## Reading a person's card

- The ruler fills to the committed time, with tentative time hatched after it, against the available time at its end.
- **capacity conflict** means committed work is more than the person's available time on at least one date.
- **Missing: availability unknown** means some dates have no calendar. Gauge does not count those dates as zero; it shows what the supplied calendars confirm instead.

Open **Daily availability and demand** for the day-by-day numbers. Choose **From** and **Through** and **Show capacity** to look at another period.
