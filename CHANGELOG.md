# Changelog

## 0.1.1 — 2026-11-26

Gauge is agency operations for owner-led design, marketing and software agencies of about 5 to 40 people. It runs in your own Cloudflare account.

Gauge prepares hosted installation with a pinned deployment descriptor, Managed mail relay support and provider setup fields. Agency operations include engagements, staffing, time approval, billing and native data export.
