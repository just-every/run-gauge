# Gauge product contract

Target edition: 17 October 2026. Gauge is a US agency professional services automation product for the owner or operations lead of a 5–40 person design, marketing, software agency or consultancy. It connects estimates and retainers, staffing, time and expenses, delivery evidence and invoicing to a live margin, utilization and cash forecast. A buyer can replace Kantata, Harvest + Forecast, Scoro or Teamwork.com, or choose Gauge over Productive, Rocketlane or Float, without maintaining a separate spreadsheet for the core agency job.

This is the destination product, not a description limited to today's release. Missing implementation is work to build, not a reduction in scope. Every clause gives the buyer behavior and an acceptance journey through a public screen, API, command, download or provider callback. Network/provider sandboxes may substitute at the paid/network edge in tests; no application mock or fabricated completion satisfies a clause. [ACCEPTANCE.md](ACCEPTANCE.md) repeats every journey and its current status. The final Status table cites implementation and tests, not a claim of live agency, provider or hosted proof.

The complete job runs from pipeline and accepted scope through retainers, phase budgets, resource requests, calendars and delivery, to approved actuals, issued invoices, collection and an explained portfolio forecast. Client collaboration, mobile/desktop time, follow-up, migration and accounting reconciliation belong to that job. PSA-specific estimates and project work remain in Gauge; neighboring products remain separate as BOUNDARY-003 specifies.

## Operating rules

- New installations start empty. The owner enters real records or imports authorized source data. Existing demo/test fixtures are never a substitute for agency migration or live-provider evidence.
- US-first means USD new-business defaults, US billing addresses and payment terms, sales-tax provider handoff, card/ACH through the agency merchant account and IANA timezones with daylight-saving handling. Exact money carries currency; durations remain whole seconds, separate from billing increments. Clients and engagements may bill in another ISO currency under SETUP-003. No implicit FX: conversion uses only SETUP-003's owner-approved, effective-dated rates, and historical AUD records never silently convert.
- Every page, read, write, API, download and callback checks current native or scoped integration authority at the handler; writes recheck grants in the committing transaction. Revocation takes effect at the next request. Browser writes check origin, credentials stay private, and restored data never imports authority.
- Every write uses a request key. Same key and content yields the original outcome; different content is refused. External commands also carry stable source/action identity and reconcile uncertain results before another attempt.
- Approval binds exact record revisions, terms, recipients and computed effects. Changed inputs require renewed approval. Approved commercial versions, frozen rates and issued artifacts keep their history; corrections append linked records with actor and reason.
- Owner holds finance authority and alone manages grants. Finance sees private rates/costs and authorizes money; leads control assigned delivery and staffing; staff confirm their own work. Detailed target roles and delegation are GOVERN-001 through GOVERN-004.
- Agents prepare; the appropriate person decides. Agent grants cannot self-approve, commit staffing, promise client changes or bill. A bounded policy can authorize only its stated follow-up action after the required human decisions. Sources and imported notes are inert data, never instructions or authority. Existing agents receive no private amounts; future least-privilege financial analysis requires a separately authorized finance scope.
- Unknown capacity, missing costs, stale connectors, pending ACH and unconfirmed evidence remain explicit. Successful submission or job invocation never proves delivery, payment or forecast recovery.

## Published operating core

The seventeen released clause IDs remain intact. The original AUD pilot now supports a saved agency currency and timezone, with USD and America/Los_Angeles defaults for new installations. Setup offers USD, AUD, CAD, EUR, GBP, NZD, CHF and SGD, all with two decimal minor units. The forward migration infers the agency currency from existing stored money without rewriting amounts, revisions or approved artifacts. Native billing remains CSV billing-packet preparation, with currency mismatches refused and no FX conversion. `done` for the published core does not mean the full SETUP-001, invoice/accounting, retainer, integration or forecasting targets are complete.

- **ENGAGEMENT-001 — Set up agreed work.** Finance creates clients and people (with work timezone and contractor flag), an engagement with its responsible lead, assigned members, agreed scope and work timezone, and its tasks and milestones. **Acceptance evidence:** `ENGAGEMENT-001 real empty account to scoped engagement`.

- **BUDGET-001 — Agree terms and change orders.** A lead or finance drafts hourly or fixed-fee terms (budget, effort, billing rounding, reason) and approves the exact version; an approved version cannot change, and a change order is a new version beside it. **Acceptance evidence:** `BUDGET-001 immutable approved terms and additive change orders`.

- **RATE-001 — Keep rates by effective date.** Finance adds person/service cost and bill rates as effective-dated, immutable versions; approving time freezes the rate that applied, so adding today's rate never rewrites past work. Rates stay private to finance. **Acceptance evidence:** `RATE-001 effective-dated private rates`; `TIME-002 return, correction and approval of exact revisions` (later rate leaves approved economics unchanged).

- **CAPACITY-001 — See next month's capacity.** People maintain dated working calendars (including contractor availability) and leave; leads allocate staff as tentative or committed. Gauge shows daily available, committed, tentative and remaining time, flags conflicts on committed work and treats a missing calendar as unknown, not zero. **Acceptance evidence:** `CAPACITY-001 working calendar, leave and separate tentative staffing`; Forecast import `tests/gauge-forecast.mjs`.

- **TIME-001 — Record time.** Staff record manual intervals or run a persistent timer, with engagement, task, service, notes, billable flag and explicit IANA timezone. Overlapping intervals and a second running timer are refused. **Acceptance evidence:** `TIME-001 persistent timer, exact manual duration, parallel exclusion and idempotence`.

- **TIME-002 — Approve and correct time.** Staff submit; the lead returns with a reason or approves the exact revision; approved time is reopened only through an audited correction and approved again. **Acceptance evidence:** `TIME-002 return, correction and approval of exact revisions`.

- **COST-001 — Record expenses with receipts.** Staff add project expenses with receipt files and a reimbursable flag; the lead returns or approves; receipts are fixed at submission and only their owner or finance can download them. **Acceptance evidence:** `COST-001 expense receipt custody, exact approval and attachment privacy`.

- **ECONOMICS-001 — Reconcile the engagement.** Finance sees the latest approved budget against approved actual time and cost, billable value and remaining effort, with the source IDs of entries missing a cost or bill rate. Others see hours without private costs. **Acceptance evidence:** `ECONOMICS-001 approved actual cost and remaining effort`.

- **BILLING-001 — Draft a billing packet.** Finance selects approved, unbilled hourly work, approved reimbursable expenses or an approved fixed-fee milestone; the draft reserves those exact source revisions, and a concurrent second reservation of the same source fails. **Acceptance evidence:** `BILLING-001 concurrent source reservations and fixed-fee eligibility`.

- **BILLING-002 — Approve and export once.** Finance approves the exact draft, which fixes its lines and CSV artifact with source IDs, revisions, durations, rates and rounding; formula-like text is escaped; repeated downloads return the same bytes; a correction is a linked adjustment packet, never an overwrite. **Acceptance evidence:** `BILLING-002 exact approval, formula-safe repeated exports and linked correction`.

- **REVIEW-001 — Review the week's exceptions.** The Review page lists missing time against calendars, overspend, capacity conflicts, approved unbilled work, missing bill rates and decisions due (including agent proposals awaiting this person), each linked to its source record. **Acceptance evidence:** `REVIEW-001 grounded decisions, missing time and capacity exceptions`.

- **ACCESS-001 — See only permitted work.** Staff see their own work on assigned engagements, leads approve only engagements they lead, private costs and rates stay with finance, and only the owner grants or revokes access; revocation takes effect on the next request, and unauthenticated or unauthorized direct transport calls are refused. **Acceptance evidence:** `ACCESS-001 native personas, scoped data and direct transport denials`; `ACCESS-001 revocation and minimal replay acknowledgements`.

- **MOVE-001 — Move in from Harvest and leave whole.** Finance previews a Harvest export package (dry run, no writes), reconciles independent client/month totals, applies it in bounded journaled batches, rolls back a partial import, re-imports without duplicates and undoes untouched imported records; edited records refuse undo. `rer export` and `rer import` restore the complete dataset into an empty install and refuse corrupt archives or occupied destinations. See [MOVE-IN.md](MOVE-IN.md). **Acceptance evidence:** `MOVE-001 Harvest preview, partial rollback, native reconciliation, idempotence and untouched undo`; `MOVE-001 complete D1 archive restore, corruption and occupied refusal`; browser journey export → fresh clone → import.

- **AGENT-001 — Time reconstruction.** An agent drafts a time entry for a person from cited, consented evidence (for example a calendar entry), noting any ambiguous client match; only that person confirms the actual interval and billability, which creates their own draft to submit. **Acceptance evidence:** `AGENT-001 time reconstruction: the agent drafts, only the person confirms actual time`.

- **AGENT-002 — Resourcing.** An agent offers up to five tentative staffing alternatives with rationale; the page shows each alternative's effect on the person's remaining capacity and conflicts (and, for finance, estimated cost and billable value); the lead approves one, which becomes a tentative allocation. Agents cannot propose committed staffing. **Acceptance evidence:** `AGENT-002 resourcing: tentative alternatives only, the lead approves one`.

- **AGENT-003 — Billing preparation.** An agent proposes the source lines for a packet; Gauge refuses unapproved, already allocated, duplicated or rate-less sources at preparation; finance approves, which drafts the exact packet, then approves the packet as in BILLING-002. The agent never sees amounts. **Acceptance evidence:** `AGENT-003 billing preparation: eligibility checked, finance approves the exact packet`.

- **AGENT-004 — Delivery exceptions.** An agent drafts a change-order brief grounded in exceptions; the lead declines it with a reason or approves it, which drafts a change-order version whose terms the lead then approves (BUDGET-001). An agent can withdraw its own open proposal. **Acceptance evidence:** `AGENT-004 delivery exceptions: change-order briefs are declined or approved by the lead`.


## Target clauses

### Agency setup and sales to delivery

- **SETUP-001 — US agency setup.** The owner sets the legal business, US billing address, USD base (reporting) currency, EIN reference, fiscal year, workweek and IANA timezones. Money uses explicit ISO currency and exact minor units; old AUD records keep their currency and never silently convert. USD is the new-install default; client and engagement billing in other currencies follows SETUP-003. **Acceptance evidence:** Start empty, configure a New York agency, quote and invoice USD work across a daylight-saving change; import an old AUD record and require an explicit conversion policy before consolidation.

- **SETUP-002 — Client and contact workspace.** Finance keeps legal clients, billing and project contacts, addresses, tax and exemption references, payment terms, PO requirements, tags and client custom fields (SETUP-004); search and merge preserve linked engagements and external identities. **Acceptance evidence:** Create two similarly named clients, merge the duplicate, search by PO and external ID, and verify terms and every linked project remain attached.

- **SETUP-003 — Client billing currencies and exchange rates.** Each client carries a default ISO 4217 billing currency (USD unless set), which an engagement may override before its first approved commercial version. Rate cards (RATE-002), estimates and proposals (SALES-002, SALES-003), retainers (RETAINER-001), invoices and credits (INVOICE-001, INVOICE-004), QBO/Xero documents (CONNECT-001, CONNECT-002) and Stripe payment links (PAYMENT-001) carry that currency end to end; one document never mixes currencies. Finance keeps an owner-approved, effective-dated exchange-rate table for each currency to the USD reporting currency, recording rate, effective date, source (the accounting provider's rate or a named published rate) and approver. Portfolio, forecast and cash figures (FINANCE-002, FINANCE-006, REPORT-001, REPORT-003) convert each amount at the rate effective on its transaction date and label the rate and date beside every converted total. An amount with no approved rate for its date stays shown unconverted, never as zero. A new or corrected rate applies forward only: it never rewrites a conversion already used in a closed period (FINANCE-004) or on an issued document. Realized FX gains and losses on settled payments remain with the accounting provider (BOUNDARY-001). **Acceptance evidence:** Set a Toronto client to CAD and a New York client to USD; approve CAD→USD 0.7300 effective 1 October; issue a CAD 10,000.00 invoice and a USD 5,000.00 invoice in October and sync both to a QBO multicurrency sandbox, where each lands in its own currency; the October portfolio total reads USD 12,300.00 labelled with the 0.7300 rate and its 1 October date. Approve 0.7400 effective 1 November and confirm the closed October total, the CAD invoice and its QBO copy are unchanged; a GBP amount dated before any approved GBP rate is shown unconverted, not zero.

- **SETUP-004 — Custom fields on clients, engagements, people and tasks.** The owner defines custom fields on clients, engagements, people and tasks, typed as text, number, currency (with its ISO currency, SETUP-003), date, single select, multi select or person. Select options can be added or retired without rewriting stored values; definitions are versioned and every value change keeps its history (GOVERN-005). A field can be required on create, which is enforced on direct creation, on DELIVERY-003 template instantiation and on SALES-004 conversion. Each field has a visibility level (everyone who can see the record, leads and finance, or finance only) enforced at the handler for pages, the API (CONNECT-020), CSV/XLSX/PDF exports, saved and scheduled reports and BI feeds (REPORT-004) and the client portal (CLIENT-001). Every field is a filter and grouping dimension in REPORT-001, REPORT-002 and REPORT-004 and a filter in RESOURCE-002 resource search; number and currency fields can be summed as report measures. Importers carry source field definitions and values with source IDs (MIGRATE-001, MIGRATE-004); a value that does not fit its field's type or options is reported in the preview, never dropped. Whole-installation export and restore (BASE-002) include definitions and values. **Acceptance evidence:** Import a Kantata OX package with a project single-select field "Practice" (Brand, Web) and a user field "Office" (NYC, Austin); the utilization and profitability reports grouped by Practice and by Office equal the totals of independent source reports from the same package, and a project carrying an undefined option "Legacy" is listed in the preview rather than dropped. Make "Practice" required on engagements and see creation with it empty refused, both directly and through a SALES-004 conversion. Add a finance-only currency field "Contract value": a lead cannot read it through the page, the API, a CSV export or a saved report, while finance sums it by Practice. Filter resource search by Office = Austin and see only Austin people.

- **SALES-001 — Opportunity pipeline.** Leads track agency opportunities with stages, probability, owner, proposed start/end, services, expected value and staffing demand; won and lost outcomes retain history. Weighted revenue and tentative capacity remain visibly distinct from signed work. **Acceptance evidence:** Create two deals at different probabilities, move one to won, and reconcile weighted pipeline and booked backlog without double counting.

- **SALES-002 — Costed estimates.** Leads model service, role, hours, subcontractor and expense lines, rate assumptions, contingency, discounts and margin for hourly, fixed and mixed work; private costs remain finance-only. Versions compare projected margin and feasible dates before sale. **Acceptance evidence:** Estimate design and engineering with a contractor and discount, compare versions and show a non-finance recipient no internal costs.

- **SALES-003 — Proposals and client acceptance.** Send a branded, versioned proposal with scope, deliverables, exclusions, dates, payment schedule and expiry; track delivery, viewing, questions and acceptance or rejection. Bind accepted terms to the exact version and attach signed evidence from Docusign or Inkwell when signatures are needed. **Acceptance evidence:** Send two proposal versions to a controlled inbox; accept the latest through the client link and require a fresh acceptance after changing its price.

- **SALES-004 — Won work becomes an engagement.** Convert accepted estimates or won CRM deals into scoped work, phase budgets, tasks, milestones, staffing requests and invoice schedules with stable links back to the deal and accepted terms; required SETUP-004 fields must be supplied or mapped from the deal before conversion; replays create no duplicate. **Acceptance evidence:** Accept a fixed-fee estimate, convert it twice, and find one engagement whose budget and milestones reconcile to that estimate.

- **SALES-005 — Change scope with the client.** Track scope requests, baseline differences, schedule and margin impact, internal review and client acceptance; no pending change consumes approved client budget or authorizes billing. Retain signed change evidence beside prior terms. **Acceptance evidence:** Propose extra work, decline it, revise and accept it as the client, then verify only the accepted increment changes the forecast.

- **RATE-002 — Client rate cards and cost policy.** Finance versions client, service, role and person rate cards, with effective dates, overtime rules, write-downs and negotiated overrides; an explain view shows precedence and approval freezes the chosen values. W-2 loaded costs and 1099 contractor costs are declared inputs, not payroll calculations. Each client rate card is denominated in that client's billing currency (SETUP-003); cost rates stay in USD. Publish precedence among entry, project, client-role, role, person and service overrides. **Acceptance evidence:** Approve identical services for two clients at different rates, cross an effective date, and trace billed value and declared loaded cost without changing historical approvals.

- **BUDGET-002 — Phase and service budgets.** Split engagements into phases and services with hour, fee, expense and margin budgets, caps and controlled transfers; rolled-up totals equal the approved engagement and warn before a cap is exceeded. The owner sets alert thresholds per engagement or phase as percentages of its hour, fee and expense budgets (default 75% and 90%), counted on approved plus submitted actuals; each threshold crossing raises one alert to the lead and finance through FOLLOWUP-003. A not-to-exceed cap on hourly work fills invoice eligibility in work-date order up to the cap: the entry that crosses it bills only its in-cap portion, and the remainder and every later approved entry are held as over-cap, never billed, until finance writes them down (RATE-002, INVOICE-004) or an accepted change order (BUDGET-001, SALES-005) raises the cap. Mixed hourly and fixed phases retain distinct billing bases. **Acceptance evidence:** Split a $60000 project across discovery and delivery, move an approved allowance and verify phase and project totals and the overspend alert. With 75% and 90% thresholds on a 100-hour phase, approving 76 hours raises one 75% alert, 15 more raise one 90% alert, and re-approving a corrected entry raises no duplicate. A $10,000 not-to-exceed hourly engagement at $150/h with 70 approved hours drafts an invoice for $10,000.00 (66 h 40 min) and holds 3 h 20 min ($500.00) as over-cap; an accepted $1,000 change order releases the $500 to the next draft, while a write-down instead removes it from billing without changing the approved time.


### Retainers and recurring commercial promises

- **RETAINER-001 — Recurring hours and value retainers.** Set monthly, quarterly or annual retainers with included hours or USD value, eligible services, proration, start/end and renewal terms. Keep each period and its commercial version separate from ad hoc project budgets. Terms explicitly choose whether all, billable or nonbillable approved work consumes allowance. A mid-period start or end prorates the period's allowance by calendar days by default, or by working days in the agency workweek and holiday calendar when the retainer says so; value prorates to the cent (half up) and hours to the nearest whole minute. **Acceptance evidence:** Create a $10000 monthly retainer and a 40-hour quarterly retainer, start mid-period and verify the previewed prorated entitlement and renewal dates: the monthly retainer starting 16 October previews $5,161.29 (16 of 31 calendar days) and renews 1 November; set to working-day proration with a Monday–Friday workweek and no October holidays it previews $5,000.00 (11 of 22 working days); the October–December quarterly retainer starting 16 October previews 33 h 29 min (77 of 92 days) and renews 1 January.

- **RETAINER-002 — Rollover and overage rules.** Version unused allowance carry-over, cap, expiry, deficit carry-forward and overage rates per retainer; show FIFO consumption and bill only allowed overages. Never change a closed period by editing current rules. **Acceptance evidence:** Carry 8 of 40 unused hours into a capped next period, consume them before expiry, exceed allowance by 3 hours and reconcile the overage at its agreed rate.

- **RETAINER-003 — Explained remaining balance.** Staff, leads and permitted clients see opening allowance, rollover, confirmed consumption, pending time, adjustments and remaining hours/value with source work and rule versions; unapproved evidence is visibly provisional. **Acceptance evidence:** Open a retainer statement, trace a consumed hour to approved time, and verify pending time changes the projection but not posted consumption.

- **RETAINER-004 — Retainer period corrections.** Close each retainer period once, preserve its statement and use approved adjustments for late time, waivers, refunds, pauses, renewal changes and cancellation; finance sees the next period and cash effect before approval. **Acceptance evidence:** Close October, receive late October work in November, approve a partial overage waiver, and show the original statement plus a linked adjustment without double consumption.

- **RETAINER-005 — Scheduled retainer billing.** Create the next recurring budget and invoice draft on its contractual schedule, in advance or arrears as configured; include overages and credits with no duplicate on restart. Finance sets the auto-issue policy and reviews exceptions. **Acceptance evidence:** Advance the public test clock over month-end twice and restart the job; obtain one new period and one correct draft, with disputed overages held.


### Staffing and resource planning

- **RESOURCE-001 — Skills and resource requests.** Keep skills, proficiency, teams, employment type, location and role needs; filter feasible people using dated availability, permissions and finance-visible cost. Placeholders represent unfilled roles until a lead approves a named person. **Acceptance evidence:** Request a 20-hour UX specialist, find qualified available staff and a contractor, fill a placeholder and verify no duplicate demand remains.

- **RESOURCE-002 — Portfolio resource schedule.** Leads plan daily/weekly allocations across projects on a calendar searchable by person, role, skill and SETUP-004 person fields, shift or split bookings and inspect tentative versus committed demand; overbooking and unknown capacity stay visible before committing. Moves retain the number of inclusive dates and the daily seconds; a split partitions those dates between two people without loss or duplication. Preview uses agency-wide committed demand, holidays and leave, with tentative demand separate; the lead explicitly applies the exact preview even when it reports overbooking or unknown capacity. Search covers current person names, roles and skills. SETUP-004 inherits person custom-field search before those fields ship. **Acceptance evidence:** Move a booking across a holiday and another project, split it between two staff and verify both conflicts and unallocated demand.

- **RESOURCE-003 — Leave and holiday approvals.** Maintain per-person part-time schedules, US federal and agency holidays, regional exceptions and requested/approved leave; private leave reasons stay private. Approval immediately recalculates conflicting staffing and alerts the responsible lead. Working hours can differ by weekday and change from an effective date without rewriting historical capacity. Approved time off can sync from BambooHR, Gusto or Rippling as leave records with stable source IDs; cancellation at the source withdraws the leave. **Acceptance evidence:** Approve leave on a staffed date, observe reduced capacity and a lead notification, and override one holiday for an agreed contractor workday. Sync the same approved BambooHR time-off twice, find one leave record, then cancel it at the source and see capacity restored.

- **RESOURCE-004 — Scheduled versus actual utilization.** Compare scheduled, recorded, submitted and approved hours by person, service and project; report billable and total utilization against net workable hours, with holidays, leave and missing calendars explained. Distinguish billable/capacity utilization from billable/scheduled utilization; label tentative inclusion. Internal non-client engagements (admin, sales, training, PTO and other internal projects) are a distinct engagement type with no client: their time counts in total utilization and never in billable utilization, and every utilization figure states its numerator and denominator. **Acceptance evidence:** Schedule 30 billable hours in a 32-hour available week, approve 24 and record 4 pending; reconcile each denominator and drill to the four categories. Log 2 hours to an internal training engagement and verify total utilization rises while billable utilization does not.

- **RESOURCE-005 — Resource demand forecast.** Forecast committed backlog and probability-weighted pipeline over months, priced on the FINANCE-006 basis with weighted pipeline as a separately labelled addition, including unfilled roles, contractors and planned hires; owner-approved scenarios compare capacity, revenue and margin without modifying the baseline. **Acceptance evidence:** Compare hire, contractor and delayed-start scenarios for a pipeline deal; close it as won and ensure demand moves to committed once.


### Project delivery and client collaboration

- **DELIVERY-001 — Task execution and views.** Teams assign tasks and subtasks with status, priority, dates, estimates, checklists, recurring work and billability; list, board and personal-work views filter by client and project. Time and expense records link to their delivery task. Task estimates and remaining effort are whole seconds of each task’s own work; subtask effort is additional. Recurrence uses an integer number of calendar days: completing a recurring root atomically creates one next task tree, shifts its dates, resets status/checklists and restores each task’s estimate as remaining effort, stopping after the inclusive last recurring start date. Exact replay creates no additional occurrence. The assignee updates progress; the engagement lead or finance assigns and edits tasks, under the existing engagement grants. Nonbillable tasks refuse billable time. **Acceptance evidence:** Create and assign a recurring task with subtasks, move it through a board and log time against it; see the same status and total in project and personal views.

- **DELIVERY-002 — Dependencies and Gantt schedules.** Plan phase, task and milestone dependencies with baselines, business-day durations, critical path and controlled rescheduling; show client date and capacity effects before changing commitments. **Acceptance evidence:** Delay an upstream task two days across Thanksgiving; inspect the dependent milestone and staffing conflicts, then approve one new baseline.

- **DELIVERY-003 — Reusable delivery templates.** Create versioned project and client-onboarding templates with task dependencies, phase budgets, forms, owners, milestone billing and required SETUP-004 custom fields; instantiate relative dates without changing running projects when a template evolves. **Acceptance evidence:** Apply a new-client template to two start dates, update its version, and confirm the earlier projects keep their original plans.

- **DELIVERY-004 — Project conversations and documents.** Comment, mention and subscribe on scoped tasks and projects; version files, attach links and search permitted content. Record decisions and deliverable approvals separately from conversation; files retain private/project/client visibility. Deliverable proofing retains version-specific annotations, reviewer requests and external approval decisions. Mentions, assignments and approval requests arrive in each person's in-app notification inbox and an optional daily or weekly email digest, alongside Slack/Teams (CONNECT-009, CONNECT-010). Projects can link Google Drive, Box or Dropbox folders and files by reference; the provider keeps their access control and Gauge stores no copy. **Acceptance evidence:** Upload two deliverable revisions, annotate a region of revision 1, mention a teammate and approve revision 2 as client-visible; the annotation stays on revision 1, the teammate sees the mention in the in-app inbox and the next digest, and a client and unrelated employee cannot fetch private revisions. Link a Google Drive folder and verify a person without Drive access is sent to Drive's own permission prompt.

- **DELIVERY-005 — Milestone acceptance and risks.** Leads track delivery health, issues, risk owner, mitigation and dated client dependencies; clients approve or reject a delivered milestone with reason. Delivery acceptance and finance authorization remain separate gates for billing. **Acceptance evidence:** Reject a milestone for missing work, resolve its issue and accept it; verify neither the initial delivery nor acceptance alone issues an invoice.

- **DELIVERY-006 — Client intake and follow-through.** Clients complete project-scoped onboarding forms, upload requested inputs and see their due actions; leads schedule reminders and escalate blocked inputs. Link broader shared-file work to Chest and accounting-practice requests to Blotter; those remain separate products. **Acceptance evidence:** Request brand assets with a due date, upload as the client, reject an incomplete submission and verify reminders stop only after acceptance.


### Time capture and expense operations

- **TIME-003 — Daily and weekly timesheets.** Workers enter day/week grids, copy recent tasks and favorites, bulk submit and see missing hours and approval status. Lock periods by policy; a historical correction preserves the original approver and exact duration before billing rounding. **Acceptance evidence:** Fill a week using a favorite and manual interval, return one day, correct it and resubmit; a locked period refuses an unapproved edit.

- **TIME-004 — Timesheet policies and reviewer queues.** Set due dates, required notes, maximum hours, billability and multi-level review policies; route work to a delegate when the lead is absent and show policy failures and approver ageing. A worker cannot self-approve when separation is required. **Acceptance evidence:** Submit short-note and excessive-hours entries, resolve the flags, delegate review during leave and verify the original worker cannot bypass the policy.

- **TIME-005 — Consented evidence inbox.** Workers explicitly connect permitted calendars and delivery projects, review suggested intervals with sources and ambiguous client matches, and confirm actual duration and billability. Revocation stops collection; activity, meetings and PRs never post approved time by themselves. **Acceptance evidence:** Connect a calendar and issue project, resolve a meeting matching two clients and reject a PR-only suggestion; revoke access and verify collection stops.

- **TIME-006 — Timer continuity and idle recovery.** One durable timer follows the same worker across web, desktop, mobile and extension; reconnects and duplicate stops preserve exact time. Idle detection and forgotten timers ask the worker how to split or discard intervals without passive surveillance. **Acceptance evidence:** Start on desktop, inspect on mobile, disconnect, reconnect and stop twice; recover an overnight timer with the worker choosing the actual interval.

- **COST-002 — Expense policy and reimbursement.** Record categories, vendor, incurred currency, approved FX evidence, tax and markup, reimbursable and client-billable status; route policy exceptions, receipt review and reimbursement exports to finance or payroll without conflating reimbursement with client recovery. Unit-priced categories (mileage per mile, per diem per day) take a quantity and an effective-dated unit rate, so a later rate change never rewrites an approved claim. **Acceptance evidence:** Submit a travel receipt with a policy exception, approve it, export it for reimbursement and invoice its allowed markup once. Claim 120 miles at the effective per-mile rate, change the rate the next day and verify the approved claim keeps its amount.

- **COST-003 — Contractor and purchase commitments.** Track subcontractor scope, PO commitments, expected vendor costs, received invoices and approval; include committed unbilled costs in forecasts and match actual costs once. Bills, purchases and expenses in QBO or Xero coded to a Gauge client or project (a QBO line's customer/project, or a Xero tracking category mapped to the project) are read back through CONNECT-001 and CONNECT-002 as actual project costs with their external IDs, matched once to any commitment; a coded bill with no mapped project is listed as unmatched. Export W-9 and 1099 classification references to the accounting provider without filing tax forms. **Acceptance evidence:** Commit $8000 to a subcontractor, receive two bills against it and reconcile forecast versus actual cost without doubling the commitment. Enter a $3,000 bill in a QBO sandbox coded to the project: after sync it appears once as actual cost against the $8,000 commitment, a re-sync adds nothing, and a bill coded to an unmapped QBO project is listed as unmatched.


### Economics, forecasts and financial control

- **FINANCE-001 — Explain actual and forecast margin.** Finance sees approved and provisional labor/expense costs, earned operational value, billed revenue, credits, paid cash, remaining effort, estimate to complete and estimate at completion per project and phase, all computed on the FINANCE-006 basis. Missing rates/costs visibly prevent a reliable margin; revenue recognition is separately labeled. **Acceptance evidence:** Approve time and expenses, leave one cost unknown, invoice half the project and trace actual/forecast margin and its incomplete flag to source records.

- **FINANCE-002 — Revenue and cash forecast.** Forecast revenue (on the FINANCE-006 basis) and expected invoices and cash by month (on the FINANCE-007 basis: invoice dates, payment terms, retainers, payment promises and collection probability); distinguish contracted backlog, weighted pipeline, issued receivables, pending and bank-settled cash, and show FINANCE-007's assumption ranges. **Acceptance evidence:** Delay a milestone by two weeks and change a payment promise; the shifted cash curve equals an independent FINANCE-007 recomputation month by month, while booked revenue and settled cash remain distinct.

- **FINANCE-003 — Project scenarios and baseline comparison.** Finance and leads compare scope, rates, staffing, write-downs and deadline alternatives in isolated scenarios; each shows dates, utilization, remaining cost, margin and cash effects across affected projects. Only named approvals adopt a scenario. **Acceptance evidence:** Model replacing a staff member with a contractor across two projects, compare baseline economics and adopt only the selected future staffing changes.

- **FINANCE-004 — Monthly period close.** Finance resolves missing time, unapproved costs, WIP, retainer usage, unbilled work and connector mismatches, then closes a period with an immutable reconciliation and role-checked reopening or later adjustment. **Acceptance evidence:** Attempt close with missing cost and an unmatched invoice, resolve both and close; late time requires a linked adjustment or authorized reopen.

- **FINANCE-005 — Revenue schedules for the accountant.** Configure service-delivery, milestone and time-based operational revenue schedules, deferred/unbilled balances and journal mappings; reconcile to invoices and send reviewed ASC 606 inputs to QBO/Xero or enterprise ERP. The accountant decides recognition policy and certification. **Acceptance evidence:** Bill a prepaid fixed project, recognize approved monthly delivery by the selected policy and reconcile the reviewed journal export to deferred and unbilled balances.

- **FINANCE-006 — Committed forecast basis.** Gauge computes every forecast per project, phase and month from one stated basis: *actual fees* = approved billable time × its frozen bill rate (RATE-001) + approved billable expenses; *estimate to complete (ETC) fees* = future **committed** allocations × the bill rate effective on each allocated day under RATE-002 precedence; *estimate at completion (EAC) fees* = actual fees + ETC fees + remaining billable expense budget; cost actuals, cost ETC and cost EAC use the same terms with the effective cost rate and the remaining expense cost budget; and *forecast margin* = EAC fees − EAC cost. Monthly forecast fees and cost place actuals in their work month, allocations on their allocated days and remaining expense budget on its stated expense dates (otherwise evenly across the phase's remaining months). Hourly work uses this formula. Fixed-fee revenue follows its approved milestone or percentage schedule (INVOICE-002), and retainer revenue follows its period schedule plus forecast overages (RETAINER-002); their cost still uses the allocation formula. Tentative allocations and probability-weighted pipeline (SALES-001) appear only as separately labelled additions ("committed", "+ tentative", "+ weighted pipeline"), never inside the committed figure. An allocation with no effective rate is listed as unpriced and marks the figure incomplete, never zero. Engagements billed in another currency convert under SETUP-003 for margin and portfolio totals. Each figure links to its formula and source rows (REPORT-005). **Acceptance evidence:** On 20 October, for an hourly engagement with cost rate $90/h and bill rate $150/h changing to $175/h on 1 November: approve 10 hours worked 1–17 October, commit 12 future hours on 21–31 October and 8 on 3–7 November, add 8 tentative November hours and a $500 billable expense budget dated November. The forecast shows actual fees $1,500, committed ETC fees $3,200, committed EAC fees $5,200, tentative-inclusive EAC fees $6,600, committed cost EAC $3,200 ($900 + $1,800 + $500) and committed margin $2,000, split October $3,300 and November $1,900 committed (+$1,400 tentative). Withdrawing the 1 November rate changes November to $1,700 and committed EAC to $5,000. A $20,000 fixed-fee project with two $10,000 milestones forecasts revenue in its two milestone months whatever its allocations.

- **FINANCE-007 — Expected cash basis.** Every cash figure (FINANCE-002, REPORT-003, RECOVER-001(d), RECOVER-003) places each receivable amount in a month by one stated basis. *Expected invoice date:* an issued invoice uses its actual issue date; unissued hourly fees and billable expenses (the FINANCE-006 actual and committed fees of each billing period) are invoiced on the engagement's billing cycle, for example monthly in arrears on the 1st of the following month; a fixed-fee milestone, percentage or deposit on its scheduled invoice date (INVOICE-002), or on its DELIVERY-005 acceptance date when acceptance is required and comes later; a retainer period on its RETAINER-005 schedule (in advance on the period start or in arrears after it), with forecast overages on the next cycle. *Expected receipt date:* expected invoice date + the client's payment terms (SETUP-002), so Net 30 on 15 October is 14 November. Finance may approve a per-client average days late, proposed from that client's settled history (RECOVER-006 payment-timing error), which is added to the due date and labelled "historical timing" with its days and approver. A recorded payment promise (INVOICE-006) sets the date for its amount until broken. An issued, unpaid invoice past its expected receipt date is placed in the as-of month and flagged overdue, never in a past month. *Expected cash:* open amount × collection probability. The open amount is the invoice total less allocated payments and credits; for unissued amounts it is the pre-tax fees plus billable expenses, labelled pre-tax. Collection probability is 100% unless finance sets a percentage for a client, an issued invoice or a scheduled invoice, and it is labelled beside the figure with the full open amount. Disputed amounts (INVOICE-006, INVOICE-007) are excluded from expected cash and listed separately with their invoice. Tentative and weighted-pipeline amounts are only the labelled additions of FINANCE-006. *Received cash:* pending ACH and processor payments (PAYMENT-001, PAYMENT-002) are shown apart from settled cash and never counted as settled. *Assumption range:* each month shows the on-terms figure and, when a days-late value is approved, the historical-timing figure; the range is the lower and higher of the two. Other billing currencies convert under SETUP-003. Each figure links to its invoice or schedule, terms, days-late value, probability and as-of time (REPORT-005). **Acceptance evidence:** On 10 October, for a New York client on Net 30 with nothing issued: a $10,000 fixed-fee milestone scheduled for 15 October, $4,000 of October hourly fees billed monthly in arrears on 1 November, and a $3,000 November retainer billed in advance on 1 November. The forecast shows November $10,000 (due 14 November) and December $7,000 (due 1 December). Approving 10 days late for the client keeps November $10,000 (24 November) and December $7,000 (11 December), labelled historical timing, with the on-terms dates as the range's other end. Moving the milestone to 29 October keeps it in November (due 28 November); moving it to 5 November moves $10,000 to December (due 5 December). Back on the original schedule, issue the milestone invoice on 15 October and record a $1,000 dispute: November expected cash reads $9,000 and the $1,000 is listed separately as disputed. Setting a 50% collection probability on the scheduled retainer invoice shows December expected cash of $5,500 beside the full $7,000. Advancing the test clock to 2 December and paying the $4,000 hourly invoice by ACH in Stripe test mode shows $4,000 pending ACH, not settled cash, until Stripe reports settlement.

## Push further

Agents use the same rights, immutable inputs and real outcome checks as people. Each proposed action names its data, authority and completion condition.

- **RECOVER-001 — Portfolio recovery agent.** Continuously join signed scope, approved changes, retainer rules, delivery evidence, capacity, rates, time/cost, invoices and payment expectations to detect drift. Open an owned case and propose a feasible cross-project recovery, comparing dates, team load, margin and cash with cited assumptions. An alternative is feasible only when all of these hold: (a) no committed allocation exceeds any person's dated capacity (CAPACITY-001, RESOURCE-003), or each over-allocation is shown as an explicit cost (overtime or contractor hours × rate) that the lead must accept; (b) task and milestone dependencies and client-dated commitments (DELIVERY-002, DELIVERY-005) hold, or each moved client date is named as a change needing client acceptance; (c) approved budgets, phase caps and retainer rules (BUDGET-002, RETAINER-002) hold, or the required change order (SALES-005) is named with its amount; and (d) its date, load and margin deltas against the current baseline are computed on the FINANCE-006 basis and its cash delta on the FINANCE-007 basis. An alternative that fails a condition without naming its cost or change is refused and never offered. **Acceptance evidence:** Delay a key deliverable on two staffed projects, ingest missing cost and compare contractor, scope and date alternatives; every proposal cites the relevant records and shows both projects effects. Plant an alternative that books a person 6 hours over their dated capacity and one that starts a task before its predecessor finishes: the first is offered only with its over-allocation cost named, the second is refused, and every offered alternative's margin delta equals an independent FINANCE-006 recomputation and its cash delta an independent FINANCE-007 recomputation.

- **RECOVER-002 — Negotiate and execute approved recovery.** With explicit lead/client/finance authority, draft and send scoped requests to staff, contractor and client, interpret replies as proposals and renegotiate only alternatives that stay feasible under RECOVER-001's definition after each reply. Approvals name exact schedule, scope, amounts, recipient and expiry; execute authorized staffing, delivery and billing changes through real connectors, escalating conflicts. **Acceptance evidence:** Approve one recovery, receive a client counteroffer and a staffing refusal, show a revised plan that passes RECOVER-001's feasibility conditions and require renewed affected approvals before connector writes; a counteroffer that would over-book the refusing person is refused, not offered.

- **RECOVER-003 — Verify recovery improved the outcome.** Read back the changed delivery schedule, allocations, approved commercial terms and invoice/payment state; recompute the forecast against the approved baseline after follow-up, with margin on the FINANCE-006 basis and cash on the FINANCE-007 basis. Close only when forecast dates, load and cash/margin improve as promised, otherwise reopen with the actual variance. **Acceptance evidence:** Simulate a successful write with unchanged provider state and refuse closure, then reconcile the real changes and demonstrate measured forecast improvement or escalate the remaining gap.

- **RECOVER-004 — Retainer promise to cash steward.** Explain each included promise, consumed work, carry-over and overage from versioned terms and delivery evidence; chase missing worker/client approvals, prepare invoices and reconcile accounting/payment outcomes. Lead approves changed promises and waivers; finance approves monetary corrections. **Acceptance evidence:** Detect an overused retainer with pending work, obtain approvals and a client overage decision, draft the correct invoice and follow it to a reconciled payment or owned exception.

- **RECOVER-005 — Evidence to completed timesheet agent.** Gather opt-in calendar and delivery evidence, ask the worker about unmatched/overlapping intervals and billability, then follow submission and approver replies to an accepted timesheet. No passive monitoring, invented durations or self-approval; revocation stops collection. **Acceptance evidence:** Offer an ambiguous two-client meeting and PR activity, accept only worker-confirmed intervals, follow a return-for-clarification and stop after the lead approves.

- **RECOVER-006 — Forecast learning with traceable assumptions.** Compare historical estimates, staffing, changes, missing costs, approved actuals and collection timing to identify bias and propose better role effort, cost and payment assumptions with confidence, cohort and sample lineage. Owner selects usable history and adopts future versions; approved terms and past work never change silently. Every proposed assumption is scored against the current one on the same held-out completed projects (at least five per cohort; a smaller cohort reports "insufficient history" and offers nothing) with three published metrics: *effort error*, the mean absolute percentage error of estimated versus approved actual hours per role; *margin error*, the mean absolute difference in percentage points between estimated and final FINANCE-006 margin; and *payment-timing error*, the mean absolute days between forecast and settled cash. A proposal is offered for adoption only when it lowers the held-out error of the quantity it changes without raising either other error, and it shows both scores, the held-out projects and the difference; otherwise Gauge shows "no improvement" with the scores and offers nothing to adopt. **Acceptance evidence:** Learn from late fixed projects, exclude one atypical client and score two proposals on the same six held-out completed projects. One lowers design-role effort error (for example from 28% to 12%) without raising margin or payment-timing error: it is offered, and once the owner adopts it only the next estimate's preview changes. The other does not beat the current assumption: it is withheld with "no improvement" and its scores. A cohort with four completed projects reports insufficient history.

- **RECOVER-007 — Signed scope to governed delivery agent.** Read approved proposal/SOW documents, extract deliverables, exclusions, retainer terms, roles and billing triggers with exact citations, flag ambiguity and propose a staffed plan and financial setup. Lead and finance review all effects before creation; signature alone never resolves ambiguous meaning. **Acceptance evidence:** Provide a signed SOW with a conflicting milestone and retainer clause, require human resolution, then approve a feasible plan whose budgets and invoice triggers match the accepted interpretation.

- **RECOVER-008 — Cited operational analyst and financial guardian.** Answer natural-language questions using role-permitted records, show formulas, sources, freshness and uncertainty, and propose actions for time-policy, margin or billing discrepancies; never expose private costs or turn an answer into authority. **Acceptance evidence:** Ask a lead and finance why utilization fell and one project lost margin; each answer cites reconciled records and permitted amounts, and a proposed correction still requires the authorized person.

- **RECOVER-009 — Migration and configuration agent.** Inspect authorized source captures, suggest mappings and setup, explain unsupported objects and reconciliation differences, and follow unresolved cases through shadow billing and the first settled cycle. Owner approves mappings/cutover and finance confirms monetary totals; the agent cannot guess missing history or approve its own mapping. **Acceptance evidence:** Present two ambiguous source users and mismatched invoice totals, resolve as the owner/finance, reconcile the shadow period and verify first-cycle invoice/payment continuity before recommending retirement of the incumbent.
