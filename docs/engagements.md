An engagement is the work a client agreed to: who leads it, who works on it, the agreed scope, its terms and its tasks. Finance and the owner create clients, people and engagements; a lead manages the terms, tasks and milestones of the engagements they lead.

## Clients and people

Add each **Client** by name. Add each **Person** with their **Work timezone** (for example `America/Los_Angeles`) and tick **Contractor** for contractors. A person is a record of who does the work; giving them a sign-in is a separate step on **Access**.

## Engagements

New engagements use the agency currency saved during setup. Existing engagements keep their stored currency. Terms, milestones, approved time economics, expenses and billing packets must use the same currency; Gauge refuses mismatches without converting amounts.

An **Engagement** has a name, a client, a **Responsible lead**, **Assigned people**, the **Agreed scope** and a work timezone. The list on **Engagements** shows each one with its approved hours on the estimate. Open an engagement to see its facts, scope, terms, tasks, milestones and economics on one page.

## Terms and change orders

**New terms / change order** drafts a version of the engagement's terms: hourly or fixed fee, the budget or fee, the estimated effort, how billable time is rounded, and the reason. **Approve terms** fixes that exact version. An approved version never changes: when the client agrees more work, add a change order as a new version beside it, and approve that.

## Tasks and milestones

Each task carries its estimated remaining effort, which feeds the engagement's economics. A fixed-fee engagement has milestones with a due date and a fee; **Approve delivered milestone** approves one once it is delivered, as long as the approved milestones stay within the agreed fee. An approved milestone can then go into a billing packet.
