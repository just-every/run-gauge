Every hour in Gauge is recorded against an engagement, a task and a service, in an explicit work timezone, and approved as an exact revision.

## Record time

On **Time**, **Manual time** records an interval: the engagement, the person, the task, the service, the **Start** and **End** date and time (read in the work timezone), the work timezone, notes and whether it is billable.

**Start persistent timer** starts a timer that keeps running until you stop it, even if you close the page. Choose **Stop timer** on the entry when you finish. Gauge refuses overlapping entries and a second running timer for the same person.

## Submit, return and approve

1. The person chooses **Submit exact revision**.
2. The engagement's lead (or finance) chooses **Approve exact revision**, or **Return for correction** with a reason.
3. A returned entry goes back to its person, who corrects it and submits again.

Approval fixes the rate and rounding that applied on the day the work was done, so adding a new rate later never rewrites approved work.

## Correct approved time

Approved time changes only through an audited correction. The lead chooses **Reopen with audited correction** and gives a reason; the entry returns to its person, who corrects it and submits it for approval again. **Review revision history** shows every earlier revision, who changed it and when. Time that is already in a billing packet cannot be reopened; correct it with an adjustment packet instead (see [Billing packets](/docs/billing)).
