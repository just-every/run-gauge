Expenses record what a project actually cost, separately from what is reimbursed to the person who paid.

## Add an expense

On **Expenses**, add the engagement, the person, the **Expense date**, a description, the amount in dollars and whether it is **Reimbursable**. Then **Add receipt** to upload the receipt file, and **Submit expense**.

## Approve or return

The engagement's lead (or finance) chooses **Approve expense**, or **Return expense** with a reason. A returned expense goes back to its person to correct and submit again.

## Receipts stay private

A receipt is fixed when the expense is submitted. Only the person who added it and finance can download it with **Download receipt**. Receipts travel with your exported data.

Approved reimbursable expenses can go into a billing packet.
