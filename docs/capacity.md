**Staffing** is a board of the next five weeks: one lane per person, this week by day and the four after it by week. Each lane shows the person's committed hours of their available hours this week, and every allocation as a bar in its client's colour.

## Reading the board

- A solid bar is **committed** work; a dashed bar is **tentative** work (a deal not yet won, a placeholder).
- **Leave** is a red hatch. Where leave falls on a day with committed work, those hours are struck through and labelled with the leave and the hours left uncovered (for example **Sick 14 hrs**): they cannot happen as booked, so the lane's committed total leaves them out. In the 5-week view that week shows the hours still worked and the struck hours as two blocks.
- A dashed outline marked **Option A** or **Option B** is an agent's proposed alternative, not yet approved.
- A flag in a week's heading marks a milestone due that week, such as a launch.
- A red dot beside a name means a conflict or leave this week; a grey dot means some dates have no calendar, so availability is unknown. Gauge does not count those dates as zero.

Use **‹** and **›** to move a week, **Today** to come back, and **5 weeks** to see every column by week.

## Staffing alternatives

When an agent prepares staffing alternatives (for example, cover for someone out sick), they appear beside the board. Each option shows its hours and dates, the person's open hours before and after, the next milestone it keeps on track or puts at risk, and, for finance, its cost against the person it covers for. The lead or finance approves one option, which adds a tentative allocation, or declines the proposal with a reason. The **Worklog** under the board lists what the agent prepared and what became of it, and the share of available hours booked this week.

## Calendars and leave

Finance adds a **Working calendar / contractor availability** for each person: the dates it covers, the available hours per working day, the working weekdays (Monday to Friday are ticked to start) and the timezone. A contractor's calendar records the availability they told you about.

Anyone can add their own **Leave**; finance can add leave for anyone. Other people see that leave was taken, not the reason.

## Allocations

A lead (or finance) adds a **Staff allocation**: the engagement, the person, the dates, the hours per date and whether it is **tentative** or **committed**. Allocations apply to every date in their range, so book working weeks Monday to Friday. Open **Staffing, leave and calendar records** to edit one.
