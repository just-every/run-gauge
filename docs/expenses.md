Expenses keep project cost, employee reimbursement and client recovery separate.

## Record and review

On **Expenses**, enter the receipt subtotal and receipt tax in the incurred currency, category, vendor and any policy exception. Choose **Client billable** and **Reimburse employee** independently. Attach the receipt and submit the exact revision.

A lead may review ordinary expenses. Finance or the owner reviews receipt-backed exceptions, unit claims, FX, tax and markup with an explicit reason. Foreign incurred cost needs the owner-approved rates effective on its date; missing rates block approval and keep provisional cost unknown. Approval freezes those rates, receipt tax and permitted markup. Markup applies to gross cost including recorded receipt tax; it changes client recovery, not reimbursement. Provider invoice sales tax remains separate.

## Mileage and per diem

Finance sets immutable dated rates under **Access**, then records a mileage or per-diem claim with a quantity (up to three decimal places). Approval selects the rate effective on the incurred date. A later rate never changes an approved claim.

## Reimburse and recover

On **Billing**, finance chooses the reimbursement specialist purpose and exact approved expenses. The manual CSV reserves each reimbursement once, separately from client invoice reservations. Totals remain separate by expense category, claim unit and currency. Downloading never sends or pays anything.

Record the receiver JSON with the exact artifact hash and every line's unchanged currency, amount, quantity and unit. Accepted and rejected outcomes remain explicitly user-recorded; a rejection needs a reason. A reconciled line can have a signed adjustment, bounded by its original approved entitlement. Continue subsequent corrections from the latest reconciled adjustment. Earlier artifacts and client invoices remain unchanged.

Only the expense owner and finance can download receipt bytes. Commercial markup and recovery figures stay with finance. The installation archive retains receipts, approved economics, rates, reimbursement reservations, artifacts and receiver history without payment authority.

The new finance review and manual reimbursement policy remains adopted pending contract approval. Gauge performs no payroll, tax filing or live accounting posting.
