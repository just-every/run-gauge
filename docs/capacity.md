**Staffing** is a board of the next five weeks: one lane per person, this week by day and the four after it by week. Each lane shows the person's committed hours of their available hours this week, and every allocation as a bar in its client's colour.

## Reading the board

- A solid bar is **committed** work; a dashed bar is **tentative** work (a deal not yet won, a placeholder).
- **Leave** is a red hatch. Where leave falls on a day with committed work, those hours are struck through and labelled with the leave and the hours left uncovered (for example **Sick 14 hrs**): they cannot happen as booked, so the lane's committed total leaves them out. In the 5-week view that week shows the hours still worked and the struck hours as two blocks.
- A dashed outline marked **Option A** or **Option B** is an agent's proposed alternative, not yet approved.
- A flag in a week's heading marks a milestone due that week, such as a launch.
- A red dot beside a name means a conflict or leave this week; a grey dot means some dates have no calendar, so availability is unknown. Gauge does not count those dates as zero.

Use **‹** and **›** to move a week, **Today** to come back, and **5 weeks** to see every column by week.

## Staffing alternatives

When an agent prepares staffing alternatives (for example, cover for someone out sick), they appear beside the board. Each option shows its hours and dates, the person's open hours before and after, the next milestone it keeps on track or puts at risk, and, for finance, its cost against the person it covers for. The lead or finance approves one option, which adds a tentative allocation, or declines the proposal with a reason. The **Worklog** under the board lists what the agent prepared and what became of it, and the share of available hours booked this week.

## Calendars and leave

Finance adds a **Working calendar / contractor availability** for each person: the dates it covers, the available hours per working day, the working weekdays (Monday to Friday are ticked to start) and the timezone. A contractor's calendar records the availability they told you about.

Enter **Holiday dates (comma-separated)** within that calendar's dates. Listed holidays remove that day's working hours before leave; Gauge does not assume federal, regional or agency holidays that you have not entered.

Anyone can add their own **Leave**; finance can add leave for anyone. Other people see that leave was taken, not the reason.

## Allocations

A lead (or finance) adds a **Staff allocation**: the engagement, the person, the dates, the hours per date and whether it is **tentative** or **committed**. Allocations apply to every date in their range, so book working weeks Monday to Friday. Open **Staffing, leave and calendar records** to edit one.

Set the allocation's service and billability to compare it with actual work. Earlier allocations without a service appear as **Unspecified**. Internal engagements have no client and their allocations and time cannot be billable. Finance creates them on Engagements with the **Internal** type and an admin, sales, training, PTO or other category.

## Scheduled versus actual utilization

Choose dates and group by person, service, project or the person/project/service detail. Scheduled means committed hours, with tentative hours shown separately. Recorded includes all stopped time; submitted is awaiting approval, and approved is final. Variance is recorded minus committed scheduled hours.

Every percentage shows its numerator and denominator. Billable and total hours divide by net calendar capacity after holidays and leave. Approved billable hours also divide by the committed schedule, with a separate ratio that includes tentative hours. Each participant's calendar counts once within a group, even across multiple services or projects. Missing calendars and zero capacity show an unknown percentage. Reports cover only work you can see.

Open **Source records** to follow staffing and each time status to its record, or inspect the calendar, holiday and leave deductions date by date. **Download utilization calculations CSV** exports the same dated figures, formulas and permitted source revisions; changed inputs require a refreshed download.

## Skills and resource requests

Finance keeps people's role, team, location, employment type and skill proficiency on Engagements. Skills use levels 1–5, for example `UX:4, Research:3`. Staffing shows the agency skills catalogue without exposing private rates.

The lead creates a role request with required skill, minimum proficiency, dates and hours per date, optionally narrowing by team, location or employment type. Every requested date needs known capacity; split requests around non-working dates. Open the role to find qualified employees and contractors. Availability deducts all committed work, holidays and leave while keeping private project details and leave reasons hidden. Missing calendars and insufficient daily capacity show a reason instead of a feasible choice.

Finance also sees the requested labor's declared cost, using the rate effective on each day. Missing rates leave cost unknown; leads receive no cost or rate details. **Approve and staff** rechecks the exact search, replaces the unfilled role with one committed named allocation and assigns the person to the engagement. Changed inputs require a new search. Replayed or racing approvals cannot duplicate the allocation. **Demand without duplicates** reconciles unfilled roles plus named staffing for the board's reporting dates.

## Billability targets and realization

Below utilization, **Billability targets and realization** shows each person's approved billable hours against their net available hours, the target in force on the last report date (a person's own target before their role's) and the variance in percentage points. Group it by person, role, client or month. Owner and finance set a target for one person or a role from an effective date with **Set a billability target**; a later target never rewrites an earlier one. Finance also sees realization: billed amounts after approved packet corrections against the standard value at each entry's frozen bill rate, write-offs, the effective bill rate per hour and approved time not yet billed. Leads and staff see hours, utilization and targets only, never amounts, rates or pay.
